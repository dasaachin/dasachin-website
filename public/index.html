The method · 4D

Problem first. Solution second.

My creative philosophy is vision over tool. AI is a medium directed by human intelligence, not a replacement for it.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    Sit with leadership to understand the business, its market, its consumers and its real challenge.

  2. 02

    Define

    Sharpen what must change and what good looks like, so there is something clear to scale.

  3. 03

    Design

    Design the AI workflow, tool or system that fits this business and no other.

  4. 04

    Deploy

    Build it into daily work and train the team to run it independently.
