The longer story

Both sides of Indian business.

Da Sachin Sharma helps businesses grow by building strong brands and putting AI to work where it actually solves a problem. With 18+ years across branding, advertising and filmmaking, he brings hands-on experience across both sides of Indian business: traditional MSME brands and new-age D2C brands.

He founded and ran his own agency, da kaizen solutions, where he served brands such as Starbucks, Reliance Life Insurance and Future Group, alongside many MSME brands, handling brand strategy, digital and social campaigns, and content production.

As Brand Head at A&A Business Consulting, he helped 200+ Indian MSMEs build, rebrand and scale across consumer electronics, food, garments and apparel. On the D2C side, he built ACwO, a consumer electronics brand, from scratch, creating its name, logo, brand identity, go-to-market strategy and positioning, and led it as Chief of Brand.

Through X DOT AI, he trains and consults brands, agencies and corporates on implementing AI across branding, marketing, content and business operations, having trained 1000+ professionals so far. His methodology is simple: problem first, solution second. He starts by understanding the business, its market, its consumers and its real challenge, then designs the AI workflow, tool or system that fits, and trains the team to run it independently.

He is Guest Faculty at IICT Mumbai and the author of five books.

The method · 4D

Problem first. Solution second.

My creative philosophy is vision over tool. AI is a medium directed by human intelligence, not a replacement for it.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    Sit with leadership to understand the business, its market, its consumers and its real challenge.

  2. 02

    Define

    Sharpen what must change and what good looks like, so there is something clear to scale.

  3. 03

    Design

    Design the AI workflow, tool or system that fits this business and no other.

  4. 04

    Deploy

    Build it into daily work and train the team to run it independently.
